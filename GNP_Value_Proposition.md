# Graphic News Plus (GNP)
## Executive Value Proposition
### Presented to Top Management — February 2026

---

> *Graphic News Plus is Graphic's digital content platform — a scalable, subscription-driven ecosystem that transforms the newspaper into a 24/7 revenue-generating digital asset.*

---

## 1. The Strategic Opportunity

The global digital news market is growing, yet many newspaper brands still monetise primarily through print. GNP is the organisation's direct answer to this shift — placing our content behind a modern, flexible digital paywall while opening new commercial channels that print simply cannot serve.

| The Old Model | The GNP Model |
|---------------|---------------|
| Print-first, digital secondary | Digital-first, always on |
| One price, one audience | Multiple tiers, many audiences |
| Revenue from newsstand & adverts | Revenue from subscriptions, B2B, affiliates, and one-time sales |
| Content consumed once | Content archived, searchable, re-sellable |

---

## 2. Core Value Pillars

### 🔐 Pillar 1 — Flexible Subscription Revenue

GNP supports a **multi-layered subscription model** designed to capture every type of reader:

- **Individual plans** — tiered pricing plans tailored to casual, regular, and power readers
- **Guest subscriptions** — frictionless sign-up to convert anonymous visitors without requiring an account
- **One-time copy purchases** — readers who won't commit to a full subscription can still buy a single edition
- **Auto-renewal** — reduces churn with built-in subscription renewal flows

> **Management Takeaway:** Multiple entry points maximise revenue capture across the widest possible audience spectrum.

---

### 🤝 Pillar 2 — B2B Commercial Partner Channel

One of GNP's most distinct revenue levers is its **Commercial Partner module** — a white-label access programme that allows corporations, institutions, and telcos to bundle GNP access for their own customers or employees.

**What this enables:**
- Onboard a corporate partner once; they bring hundreds or thousands of subscribers
- Partners manage their own subscriber lists through a dedicated **Partner API** with API-key authentication
- Paystack subaccount integration means partner revenue is automatically split and reconciled
- GNP admins retain full visibility of partner performance through subscriber summaries and stats dashboards

> **Management Takeaway:** A single enterprise partnership can generate subscription volume equivalent to thousands of individual sign-ups — at a fraction of the customer acquisition cost.

---

### 📢 Pillar 3 — Affiliate Marketing Programme

GNP includes a fully operational **Affiliate Marketing engine**, enabling individuals and influencers to earn commissions by driving subscriptions.

- Affiliates earn commissions tracked per-sale at the platform level
- Commission history is available per affiliate in real-time
- Payouts can be issued individually or in **bulk** to all affiliates with outstanding balances
- Affiliate accounts are automatically provisioned with credentials and notified via welcome email upon registration

> **Management Takeaway:** Turns loyal readers and content creators into a self-funding sales force — zero upfront marketing spend for acquired subscribers.

---

### 📰 Pillar 4 — Scalable Content Ingestion & Management

The platform is built around a robust **content pipeline** that manages the full lifecycle of a newspaper edition:

1. **Ingest** a new newspaper edition (manually or via scheduled ingestion jobs)
2. **Publish / Unpublish** with a single API call
3. Separate **free vs. paid** content tiers per publication
4. Multiple **publication brands** can be managed from a single platform instance
5. **View analytics** tracked per edition to inform editorial decisions

#### 🔍 OCR-Enabled Intelligent Search

A standout capability of the ingestion engine is its **built-in Optical Character Recognition (OCR) pipeline**. Every newspaper edition ingested into GNP is automatically processed through an OCR layer that extracts the full text content from the edition.

This powers a platform-wide **keyword search experience** — readers can search for any topic, name, or story across the entire newspaper archive and jump directly to the relevant section, without scrolling through the entire edition.

**Why this matters for engagement:**

| Without OCR Search | With GNP OCR Search |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Reader must scan every page manually | Reader searches a keyword and lands on the exact story |
| Casual readers often give up before finding content | Instant relevance keeps readers on-platform longer |
| Content discovery limited to the front page | Deep archive content becomes discoverable and valuable |
| Difficult to justify subscription for busy users | Time-poor professionals find exactly what they need in seconds |

This feature directly improves **platform stickiness** — readers who find value quickly return more often, and the ease of targeted reading becomes a core reason to maintain (and renew) a subscription.

> **Management Takeaway:** GNP is not just a reader-facing product — it is a complete digital publishing operation layer that reduces manual work, enables editorial scale, and transforms the archive into a living, searchable knowledge asset that keeps subscribers coming back.

---

### 🎯 Pillar 5 — Marketing & Campaign Tools

The platform ships with a built-in **campaign management** system to drive promotional activity without third-party tools:

- Create, target, and publish promotional campaigns directly within GNP
- **Coupon engine** — issue discount codes tied to plans, expiry windows, and usage limits
- **Scheduled campaign execution** — campaigns run automatically at the configured time
- Full campaign lifecycle management: create → publish → archive

> **Management Takeaway:** Promotional agility is built-in — marketing teams can run time-sensitive campaigns (e.g., public holiday offers) without engineering involvement.

---

### ⚡ Pillar 6 — Real-Time Communication

GNP features a **Notifications Hub** — a live broadcast channel between the platform and all connected users.

- Instantly push alerts for subscription status, payment confirmations, and breaking editorial announcements
- Supports broadcast to all active sessions simultaneously
- Zero additional infrastructure required — built directly into the API

> **Management Takeaway:** Keeps readers engaged and informed in real-time, increasing session frequency and reducing payment-related support inquiries.

---

### 📱 Pillar 7 — Progressive Web App (PWA)

GNP is delivered as a **Progressive Web App** — meaning readers get a native app-quality experience directly from their browser, on any device, with no app store required.

- **Install to home screen** — readers can add GNP to their phone or desktop like a native app, one tap, zero friction
- **Offline reading** — previously loaded editions remain accessible even without an internet connection, critical for readers in low-connectivity areas
- **Push notifications** — the platform can surface breaking news alerts and subscription reminders natively on the device
- **No app store dependency** — updates ship instantly without waiting for App Store or Play Store review cycles, and there is no 30% platform commission on in-app purchases
- **Cross-platform by default** — one codebase works seamlessly on Android, iOS, and desktop

| App Store Model | GNP PWA |
|-----------------|---------|
| 30% commission on purchases | 0% platform fee — full revenue retained |
| Weeks to push updates | Instant over-the-air updates |
| Separate iOS and Android builds | One universal experience |
| Users must find and install the app | One tap from any web link |
| Offline requires heavy native build | Built-in on the web platform |

> **Management Takeaway:** GNP delivers the richness of a native mobile app without the cost, delay, or revenue share imposed by the app stores — lowering the barrier to adoption while protecting our margins.

## 4. Platform Security & Trust

GNP is built with enterprise-grade security from the ground up:

| Security Feature | Detail |
|-----------------|--------|
| **JWT Authentication** | Every protected endpoint requires a signed token |
| **Role-Based Access** | Separate login flows for readers, admins, and affiliates |
| **Passkeys (WebAuthn)** | Passwordless login option — industry-leading auth standard |
| **OTP Verification** | Two-factor account confirmation flow |
| **API Key Management** | Partners access B2B endpoints via revocable, auditable API keys |
| **Account Controls** | Admin ability to lock, unlock, activate, or deactivate any account instantly |

> **Management Takeaway:** GNP is built to handle sensitive financial and identity data safely, meeting the expectations of enterprise partners and security-conscious subscribers.

## 5. Technology Advantage

GNP is built on **C++ with the Drogon framework** — one of the highest-performance HTTP server ecosystems available.

| Metric | Benefit |
|--------|---------|
| **Async-first architecture** | Handles thousands of concurrent requests without performance degradation |
| **Low resource footprint** | Reduced cloud infrastructure cost vs. equivalent Node.js or Python APIs |

| **CORS-configured** | Seamlessly integrates with the GNP web frontend with zero cross-origin friction |
